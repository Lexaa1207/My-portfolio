Trade & Logistics

How Import & Export Works

A cross-border shipment moves through planning, documentation, customs, and delivery.

01 / PLAN

Know the shipment

Confirm goods, destination, trade restrictions, likely duties, and the agreed Incoterm responsibilities.

02 / DOCUMENT

Prepare paperwork

Align the commercial invoice, packing list, transport documents, and any certificates or permits needed.

03 / SHIP

Declare & dispatch

Arrange freight and submit accurate export details, including description, classification, origin, and value.

04 / DELIVER

Clear & receive

Complete destination import clearance, settle applicable charges, release the goods, and confirm delivery.
